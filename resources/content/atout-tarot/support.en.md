## FAQ

### How do I start a game?

Tap **New game**, pick the players (or add new ones), put them in table order and choose who deals
first. With 6 players or more, choose how many play each hand: the app shows who sits out.

### How do I enter a hand?

Pick the taker, the contract, the called king (with 5 players), the number of oudlers and the points.
You can type the taker's or the defence's points, half points included. Petit au bout and handfuls
are in the options. The app shows the result before you confirm.

### I made a mistake in a hand

Tap the hand on the score sheet to edit or delete it: every total is recalculated. You can also undo
the last action.

### How are points worked out?

By the French Tarot Federation (FFT) rules: points needed by oudlers (56, 51, 41 or 36), 25 base
points plus the margin, multiplied by the contract (×1, ×2, ×4, ×6), plus Petit au bout and handfuls.
Half-point rounding is set when you create the game.

### Where are my games?

On your phone only. There is no account and no server: if you delete the app, your games are erased
(unless they are part of a backup of your phone).

### I bought Premium on another phone

Open **Settings → Restore purchases**, with the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

### How do I change my ad choices?

In **Settings → Ad privacy choices**, you can open the consent form again. On iPhone, tracking permission is
in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
