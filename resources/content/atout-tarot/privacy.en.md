# Atout Tarot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Atout Tarot ("the app") is a score sheet for the card game French Tarot, published by {publisher}
("we"). This page explains which data the app uses and why.

## In short

- No account, no sign-up.
- Your games, players and settings stay **on your phone**. We never receive them.
- The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Google may use your device's advertising identifier
  **only if you allow it**.
- Purchases go through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

## 1. Data you enter

Player names (or nicknames), hands, scores, stats and your settings are stored in a local database
on your device. They are not sent to us or to any third party. They may be included in your phone's
backups (iCloud or Google), depending on your own settings.

When you share a result (text or image), you choose the app and the recipient. What you share is
then subject to that app's rules.

To erase everything: delete the players and games in the app, or uninstall it.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). No ad is
requested while you enter a hand or on the score sheet.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

- the device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Android advertising ID), if you allow it;
- the IP address, from which Google may infer an approximate location (country, city);
- technical information about the device and the app (model, OS, language, version);
- ad interactions (impressions, taps) and SDK diagnostics.

**Your consent.**

- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form (Google UMP, IAB TCF
  compliant) asks for your choice before any ad is shown. You can change it at any time in the app's
  **Settings**.
- On iOS, the app then asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline,
  the IDFA is not used.
- If you decline personalisation, Google shows non-personalised ads, which still use limited data
  (for example the IP address for approximate location and fraud prevention).

On Android you can also delete or reset your advertising ID in the phone's settings. To learn how
Google uses data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

With **Premium**, the app shows no ads and makes no ad requests.

## 3. In-app purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Payment and
billing details are processed by them under their own policies. The app only receives confirmation
of the purchase and remembers on the device that Premium is active. It may check the purchase with
the store at launch (for example for "Restore purchases").

## 4. Other network connections

The app works offline. It only connects for:

- ads (see §2) and purchases (see §3);
- the "Our other apps" list, a file downloaded from our server. As with any web page, the server
  sees your IP address and nothing else;
- app updates: besides the stores, the app may download updates of its code from the Bifrost
  service (NativePHP). The request contains the app's identifier and version, not your data.

We use no analytics or tracking tools other than the ads SDK.

## 5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

## 6. Your rights

We hold no personal data about you: everything is on your device. For data processed by Google for
advertising, you can exercise your rights (access, erasure, objection) with Google, and change your
consent at any time in the app. You can also contact your data protection authority (in France, the
CNIL: <https://www.cnil.fr>).
