## FAQ

### How do I get started?

Create your team and add your players in the **Teams** tab. Then, in the **Games** tab, tap **+** to
create a game: opponent, date, format (4 × 10 min FIBA, 4 × 8 min youth, 2 × 20 min…) and available
players.

### How do I track stats during the game?

Pick your starting lineup, start the clock, then tap an action (made or missed shot, rebound,
assist, foul…) and the player involved. The clock stops automatically on fouls and timeouts.

### I made a mistake

Tap **Undo** to remove the last action. For an older one, open the history at the top of the
screen: you can edit or delete any action there.

### How do I make a substitution?

During the game, tap **Substitutions**, then the player going out and the one coming in. Each
player's minutes and +/- are computed automatically.

### Where is my data stored?

On your device, and in your iCloud account if you're signed in. Hooparo has no server: nobody but
you can access it. Without iCloud, the app works normally and your data stays on the device. More
details in the [privacy policy](/en/hooparo/privacy).

### Does my data sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, if both devices use the same iCloud account. The cloud icon at the top of the Games tab shows
the sync status, and each game shows whether it's already in iCloud or waiting to be sent. The app
also works offline: your changes are sent as soon as you're back online.

### Sync isn't working

Make sure you're signed in to iCloud (Settings > [your name]) and that Hooparo is allowed in the
list of apps using iCloud. Tap the cloud icon to see the status details.
