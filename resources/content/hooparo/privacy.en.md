## Who we are

## Data we collect

None. Hooparo requires no account and uses no server of its own. The app contains no ads, no
analytics and no trackers, and shares no information with third parties.

## Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores on your iPhone or iPad the information you enter: team names, players' first
and last names, jersey numbers and notes, player photos, games and stats. We never have access to it.

This data may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), depending on your own
settings. These backups are handled by Apple under its privacy policy.

## iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud on your device, Hooparo syncs this information with the private
database of your iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit service), so it is backed up and available on your
other devices. This data is stored by Apple, in your iCloud storage, under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Only your iCloud account can access
it: neither we nor any third party can see it.

Without an iCloud account, or if you turn off iCloud for Hooparo in Settings, your data stays on
your device only.

## Camera and photos

Camera access is only requested if you choose to take a picture of a player. When you pick an
existing photo, Apple's picker only shares the selected photo with the app, not your whole library.
Photos stay on your device and in your iCloud account.

## Minors

The app can be used to track youth teams. Player information stays on the device of the person who
enters it (and in their personal iCloud account) and is not sent to anyone else. We recommend
getting parental consent before saving a photo of a minor.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any game, player or team from within the app at any time; the deletion also applies
to your iCloud copy and your other devices.

Deleting the app erases its data from your device, but not the copy kept in iCloud. To erase it,
open Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Hooparo, then delete the data.
